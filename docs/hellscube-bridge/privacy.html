hellscube-bridge Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 13, 2026

hellscube-bridge is a Reddit Devvit app used by subreddit moderators to post card acceptance images on Reddit on behalf of an authorized backend service.

Data we process

  • Post title, image URL, optional flair ID, and target subreddit name, sent by an authorized backend using a shared secret.
  • Public HTTPS image URLs (for example, storage.googleapis.com) fetched temporarily to submit a Reddit image post.
  • Reddit API data needed to create posts as the installed app account.

What we do not do

  • We do not operate a public sign-up site or collect accounts from Reddit users.
  • We do not sell personal data or use advertising trackers.
  • We do not fetch arbitrary third-party URLs; only hosts allow-listed in the app configuration.

Who controls the app

The app is installed and configured by subreddit moderators. Backend credentials and global app settings are managed by the app operator, not by ordinary Reddit users.

Data retention and deletion

Post content lives on Reddit according to Reddit’s policies. To remove a post, use normal Reddit moderation tools. For questions about this app, contact the installing subreddit’s moderators.
